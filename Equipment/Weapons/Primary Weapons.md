# PRIMARY WEAPONS

*Players can choose one Tier 1 primary weapon during character creation.  
The GM can make other weapons available throughout the campaign as the PCs level up.*  

| Tier | Levels |
|:----:|:------:|
|  1   |   1    |
|  2   |  2-4   |
|  3   |  5-7   |
|  4   |  8-10  |
