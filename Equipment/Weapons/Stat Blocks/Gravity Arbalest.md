*Tier 4 Primary Magic Weapon*

| TRAIT | RANGE | DAMAGE | BURDEN |
|---|---|---|---|
| Knowledge | Far | d8+6 mag | Two-Handed |

***Magnetic:*** When you make an attack with this weapon, you can spend a Hope to force all adversaries within Very Close range of the target to make a Reaction Roll (16). Creatures who fail must mark a [[Stress]] and are pulled into Melee range of the target.
