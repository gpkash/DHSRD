> Make a **[[Spellcast Rolls|Spellcast Roll]]** against a target within Close range. On a success, they become temporarily *Enraptured*. While *Enraptured*, a target's attention is fixed on you, narrowing their field of view and drowning out any sound but your voice. Once per rest on a success, you can **mark a [[Stress]]** to force the *Enraptured* target to mark a [[Stress]] as well.
