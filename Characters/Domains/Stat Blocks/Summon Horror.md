> Once per scene, **mark a [[Stress]]** to summon an otherworldly creature that deals **d8+1** magic damage using your Spellcast trait to a target within Far range. If the target marks any Hit Points from this attack, they must succeed on a **Reaction Roll (12)** to steel themselves against the horror or mark an equal number of [[Stress]].
> 
> After making the attack, the creature dissipates.
