> You can peek into the minds of others. **Spend a Hope** to read the vague surface thoughts of a target within Far range. Make a **[[Spellcast Rolls|Spellcast Roll]]** against the target to delve for deeper, more hidden thoughts.
> 
> On a roll with [[Fear]], the target might, at the GM's discretion, become aware that you're reading their thoughts.
