> Once per rest, you can conjure a thick, twisting stalk within Close range that can be easily climbed. Its height can grow up to Far range.
> 
> **Mark a [[Stress]]** to use this spell as an attack. Make a **[[Spellcast Rolls|Spellcast Roll]]** against an adversary or group of adversaries within Close range. The erupting stalk lifts targets you succeed against into the air and drops them, dealing **d8** physical damage using your Proficiency.
