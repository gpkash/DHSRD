> Make a **[[Spellcast Rolls|Spellcast Roll]]** against all targets within Far range. Targets you succeed against become temporarily *Enraptured*. While *Enraptured*, a target's attention is fixed on you, narrowing their field of view and drowning out any sound but your voice. **Mark a [[Stress]]** to force all *Enraptured* targets to mark a [[Stress]], ending this spell.
