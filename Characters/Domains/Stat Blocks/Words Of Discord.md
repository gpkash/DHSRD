> [!domain-grace]+ ![[grace.png|18]] WORDS OF DISCORD
> 
> ![[Words Of Discord.jpeg]]
> 
> **5**
> 
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **Spell** | **1⚡** |
> 
> Whisper words of discord to an adversary within Melee range and make a **[[Spellcast Rolls|Spellcast Roll]] (13)**. On a success, the target must mark a [[Stress]] and make an attack against another adversary instead of against you or your allies.
> 
> Once this attack is over, the target realizes what happened. The next time you cast Words of Discord on them, gain a -5 penalty to the [[Spellcast Rolls|Spellcast Roll]].
