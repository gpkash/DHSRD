> **Spend 2 Hope** to conjure an eerie mist that turns you and allies of your choice within Close range momentarily incorporeal. While a creature is incorporeal, they can move through solid objects and are immune to physical damage. They become corporeal again after they pass through a solid object or make an action roll. Otherwise, this effect lasts until the end of the scene.
