> [!domain-sage]+ ![[sage.png|18]] NATURAL FAMILIAR
> 
> ![[Natural Familiar.jpeg]]
> 
> **2**
> 
> | | |
> |---|---|
> | **Spell** | **1⚡** |
> 
> **Spend a Hope** to summon a small nature spirit or forest critter to your side until your next rest, you cast Natural Familiar again, or the familiar is targeted by an attack. If you **spend an additional Hope**, you can summon a familiar that flies. You can communicate with them, make a **[[Spellcast Rolls|Spellcast Roll]]** to command them to perform simple tasks, and **mark a [[Stress]]** to see through their eyes.
> 
> When you deal damage to an adversary within Melee range of your familiar, you add a **d6** to your damage roll.
