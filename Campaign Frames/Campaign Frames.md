# CAMPAIGN FRAMES

A campaign frame is a pre-built starting point for a Daggerheart campaign — a setting pitch, tonal guideposts, mechanical hooks, and a session-zero scaffold that a table can pick up and run with, or pull apart for inspiration.

## COMPLEXITY RATING

Each frame carries a complexity rating (one to five dots). Lower-complexity frames sit comfortably inside Daggerheart's default high-fantasy genre and add few extra rules. Higher-complexity frames push the genre — sci-fi, horror, post-apocalypse — and introduce homebrew-adjacent mechanics; they reward GMs who are comfortable inventing stat blocks, equipment, and bespoke systems on the fly.

## SECTIONS IN EACH FRAME

Every frame in this chapter follows the same outline:

- **The Pitch** — short read-aloud to introduce the campaign to players.
- **Tone & Feel** — adjectives capturing the vibe.
- **Themes** — the questions and tensions the campaign is *about*.
- **Touchstones** — books, films, games to reference for shared shorthand.
- **Overview** — the campaign's background and current state of the world.
- **Communities, Ancestries, Classes** — guidance on how each fits the setting.
- **Player Principles & GM Principles** — guideposts to stay in tone.
- **Distinctions** — setting-specific distinctions (terrain, factions, items, supernatural elements).
- **The Inciting Incident** — the kickoff event for session one.
- **Campaign Mechanics** — frame-specific rules (countdowns, resources, special checks).
- **Session Zero Questions** — prompts the table works through together.

## THE FRAME-TO-CAMPAIGN PROCESS

A frame is meant to bootstrap your campaign in five steps:

1. **Pitch the campaign** — read the pitch, share tone/themes/touchstones, gauge buy-in.
2. **Run session zero** — work through the frame's questions and the standard Daggerheart [[Session Zero and Safety Tools]].
3. **Make characters** — use the frame's community/ancestry/class guidance to anchor characters in the setting.
4. **Build the map** — pass the frame's map around the table; players add locations together.
5. **Run the inciting incident** — open session one with the frame's prebuilt hook.

## HOPE & FEAR CAMPAIGN FRAMES

*When starting a Daggerheart campaign, you might wish to begin with a campaign frame. Four campaign frames are presented here in order of complexity.*

### [[Castle High]]

*In a modern fantasy world, teenagers attend a boarding school in the clouds—sneaking out of class to fly brooms, explore floating islands, and save their friends.*

Designed by Rowan Hall, Mikey Hamm, Cass Rea, and Spenser Starke

**Complexity Rating:** •

**The Pitch:**

In a modern world of knights and cars, wands and flip phones, there's no better place to study magic than the floating school known as Castle High. Drifting among the mysterious ruins of the ancient world and modern settlements that populate the clouds, this school and its surroundings hold secrets, danger, and something more sinister. A string of disappearances has recently plagued the school, and no one knows who or what is behind it—or who will disappear next.

In a Castle High campaign, you'll play adventurous high schoolers who take magic classes, explore sky islands, and search for their missing classmates.

*This campaign frame starts on page 116.*

### [[Reign of the Weredragon]]

*In a land ruled by a violent weredragon tyrant, heroes ordained by the stars to defeat her must rise to the challenge and defend their people.*

Designed by Jeremy Crawford, Rowan Hall, Christopher Perkins, and Spenser Starke

**Complexity Rating:** ••

**The Pitch:**

Once upon a time, in the peaceful land of Whelvia, a powerful sorceress named Forlorne Lykona deceived the country's great dragon warden to absorb his power and rule the kingdom herself. Transformed into a weredragon by the powerful ritual that fused the warden's power with her cold heart, Forlorne now takes to the skies, feasting on all who challenge her and cursing innocent villagers to become ravenous weredrakes. The people called on the brave defenders of the realm, the Knights of Whelvia, to protect them—but the knights instead bent the knee to the draconic tyrant, allowing her to command their forces and leaving the land without protectors.

In a Reign of the Weredragon campaign, you play destined heroes of Whelvia, rising up against Forlorne Lykona and her knights to save this once-idyllic land from the jaws of tyranny.

*This campaign frame starts on page 128.*

### [[Dark Heart of Andaluria]]

*As the nights grow long in a land of rich wine and ancient superstitions, a band of monster hunters battles the creatures that stalk the shadows.*

Designed by Carlos Cisco, Rue Dickey, Rowan Hall, and Rob Hebert

**Complexity Rating:** •••

**The Pitch:**

Andaluria is a land of sweeping vineyards where beauty and dread intertwine. By day, the streets and valleys are alive with festivals, music, and the scent of wine—but when the sun sets, monsters come out to feast. For generations, the old superstitions and folk rites kept the beasts at bay, but the nights have grown unnaturally long, and the citizens of Andaluria live in fear of increasingly terrifying monsters.

In a Dark Heart of Andaluria campaign, you'll play monster hunters who must investigate horrific crimes, navigate political strife, and slay supernatural creatures to protect the people from eternal darkness.

*This campaign frame starts on page 144.*

### [[Journey to Horizon]]

*After a thousand-year cataclysm, a fellowship of heroes embarks from their magically protected sanctuary to journey across a broken continent and resurrect the fallen goddess who can heal their world.*

Designed by James Haeck, Rob Hebert, James Introcaso, and Spenser Starke

**Complexity Rating:** ••••

**The Pitch:**

Long ago, the vast continent of Valloren was home to wondrous landscapes shaped by the goddess Aurelia, wielder of the elemental essence called terrana. But when her disciples' betrayal threatened to extinguish magic forever, Aurelia sacrificed her own life to call down the stars and purge the land of corruption. To protect her people from annihilation, Aurelia raised magical barriers that would last a thousand years.

In a Journey to Horizon campaign, you play heroes chosen by fate to leave their recently unsealed sanctuary and travel across a turbulent and hostile world on a quest to resurrect the goddess.

*This campaign frame starts on page 158.*
