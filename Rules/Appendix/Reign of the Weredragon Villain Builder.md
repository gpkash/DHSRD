# REIGN OF THE WEREDRAGON
## VILLAIN BUILDER

*Cut out the prompts, fold them up, randomly select fifteen prompts, and place them on the table within reach of everyone participating. Go around the table, starting with the player to your left. On each player's turn, they grab a prompt, read it aloud, then answer the question. Other players may follow up with additional questions, though the player with the prompt doesn't need to answer them.*

*If the player chooses a prompt they would rather not answer, or would prefer somebody else answer instead, they can always pass it to that player or discard it from play. When there are no more prompts to answer, continue to character creation.*

1. The queen once ordered the creation of something beautiful in her honor. What was it, and where does it now reside?
2. What unique magical item is Forlorne rumored to seek? What are people afraid she'll do if she gets it?
3. Forlorne holds an annual celebration in her own honor. What does she require from citizens during the event, and how are they punished if they refuse to comply?
4. What inoffensive item, symbol, or practice did Forlorne ban when she came to power? What is the punishment for those who are caught violating the ban?
5. What is a unique or unexpected part of Forlorne's appearance that affects how people view her?
6. What defensive systems are rumored to protect the fortress of Nwarlyth where Forlorne resides?
7. Forlorne and one of the Knights of Whelvia are rumored to be having an affair. Who is the knight, and what are they known for?
8. Who is the queen's most recently appointed royal advisor, and what aspect of the kingdom are they responsible for?
9. What heinous acts has Forlorne imposed upon her citizens since becoming queen?
10. What is the Whelvians' nickname for Forlorne, and where did it come from?
11. Forlorne recently quashed a small rebellion. Who led that effort, and what mistake did they make that led to their downfall?
12. The queen's favorite weapon once belonged to someone else. What is it, and who owned it previously?
13. Forlorne frequently visits a remote Whelvian village. Why do the locals believe she keeps coming back?
14. Which Whelvian hero vocally supports Forlorne? How does she reward them?
15. The queen's regime has taken many people from their homes. Why were these people chosen, and where does Forlorne send them?
16. What distinct markings, patterns, or features do Forlorne's weredrakes bear once she transforms them?
17. The queen recently lost a close member of her royal court. Who were they, and how has she responded to their death?
18. The queen has a childhood friend who's now a member of her court. Who are they, and what's strange about them?
19. What creature does the queen keep chained in her dungeon, and why does she keep it alive?
20. What scandalous story is everyone telling from Forlorne's last grand ball?
21. One member of Forlorne's royal court is treated differently from the others. Why is that, and how do the other nobles feel about it?
22. Forlorne sometimes disappears from Whelvia for short stretches of time. Where do people say she goes?
23. What trait or behavior of others brings out Forlorne's cruelty?
24. Someone saved the queen's life. Who were they, and what happened?
25. Forlorne recently destroyed something vital to many Whelvians. What happened, and who has been most affected?
26. Forlorne's castle contains a secret room that only she can access. What does she hide there?
27. What act of kindness did Forlorne recently commit? What controversy has come about because of it?
28. What promises has Forlorne made to the people of Whelvia, and what, if any, action has she taken to fulfill them?
29. A foreign leader recently asked Forlorne for her hand in marriage, hoping to forge a powerful alliance. Who were they, and how did Forlorne respond?
30. Forlorne is demonizing a specific community, blaming them for Whelvia's problems. Which community is it, and what has she tasked her knights to do in response?
31. Forlorne meets her sky seers in a very specific place. Where do they go, and why does she bring them there?
32. What specific claims does the queen's most recent propaganda campaign make to her people?
33. What symbol do followers of the queen use to identify themselves to one another?
34. What symbol do Whelvian rebels use to identify themselves to one another?
35. Forlorne elevated someone to power who was an unexpected choice. Who are they, and why do people believe she chose them?
36. What new process has Forlorne implemented to dispatch those she deems criminal in Whelvia?
37. Forlorne recently killed a sky seer. What did this person see in the night sky that caused her to kill them?
38. What did Forlorne say at her last widely attended speech? How did the audience react?
39. Which criminal organization handles clandestine tasks for the queen?
40. What is the queen's rumored vice, and where in Whelvia does she go to satisfy it?
