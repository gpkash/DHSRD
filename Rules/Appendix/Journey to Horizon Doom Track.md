# JOURNEY TO HORIZON
## DOOM TRACK

The **Doom Track** represents growing disasters that befall New Valloren if the party doesn't resurrect Aurelia. At the end of each session, mark a box from the party's tier or below. You can't mark a nested box until you mark the box it's nested under.

## TIER 1: DARK HARBINGERS

After you mark 5 boxes in this tier, when you gain [[Fear]] at the end of a long rest, you roll twice and take the higher result.

- [ ] Bandits and marauders harry travelers.
    - [ ] Tensions rise over contested resources.
    - [ ] A mysteriously empty sanctuary is discovered.
    - [ ] A sanctuary seals itself off once again.
- [ ] A new settlement is overtaken by surging wilderness.
    - [ ] Chimeric dire beasts reveal their presence.
    - [ ] Swarms of overgrown insects darken the sky.
    - [ ] Wild magic shatters magic and crumbles stone.
    - [ ] Terrana supercharges magic dangerously and unpredictably.
- [ ] A shadowblighted orchard turns to ash overnight.
    - [ ] Desiccated and blackened birds fall dead from the sky.
    - [ ] Shadowblighted echoes of lost loved ones appear.
- [ ] Travelers trade stories of Seeker sightings.
    - [ ] The party suffers visions of the Lovers' re-emergence.
    - [ ] An Aurelian shrine lies broken and desecrated.
    - [ ] The party finds signs of a Seeker.
    - [ ] The party finds remnants of a cult ritual.
    - [ ] A cultist spy tracks the party.
    - [ ] The Vessel attracts attention—and suspicion.

## TIER 2: RISING THREATS

After you mark 5 boxes in this tier, when you generate a region and the result on the d20 is a 1, treat it as if you'd rolled 1 twice in a row.

- [ ] Roving bands of professional soldiers demand tribute at swordpoint.
    - [ ] A demagogue rises to power with promises of prosperity.
    - [ ] A noble demands fealty from their neighbors.
    - [ ] A prominent figure in the Aurelian Order persecutes the unfaithful.
    - [ ] Civilians are drafted in preparation for war.
- [ ] Chaotic weather kills the harvest.
    - [ ] Tidal waves and tornadoes batter Valloren.
    - [ ] Dire beasts and walking carnivorous plants attack.
    - [ ] A fungal plague spreads through the land.
    - [ ] Food stores are spoiled by pestilence.
    - [ ] The ground cracks open, releasing a toxic miasma.
- [ ] Shadowblight swamps a sanctuary, trapping its inhabitants.
    - [ ] A reservoir's water turns into tar.
    - [ ] Sorrowblighted humanoids emerge from hidden settlements.
- [ ] A Seeker crosses the party's path.
    - [ ] An irreplaceable repository of Old Valloren knowledge is destroyed.
    - [ ] Numerous Aurelian priests are slain in a coordinated attack.
    - [ ] A Seeker chases the party, forcing them to flee.
    - [ ] An enemy discovers the Vessel's true nature.

## TIER 3: IMMINENT DESTRUCTION

After you mark 5 boxes in this tier, adversaries gain the following feature:

***Dark-Hearted - Passive:*** **Spend a [[Fear]]** to give the adversary advantage on its next roll.

- [ ] War is declared, and each sanctuary is called to claim a side.
    - [ ] A sanctuary falls to internal strife.
    - [ ] A vast military force sweeps through the continent.
    - [ ] A sanctuary is besieged, then falls to military force.
- [ ] An explosion of verdant abundance renders regions impassable.
    - [ ] An ancient creature awakens from millennia of dreamless slumber.
    - [ ] Unleashed terrana drives people into animalistic fury.
    - [ ] Spellcasting triggers magical exhaustion.
    - [ ] Beasts of legend leave their lairs to terrorize the population.
- [ ] Color drains from the world.
    - [ ] Victims of the Long Sorrow crawl from their graves.
    - [ ] Entire fields, forests, jungles, and wetlands fall to rot.
    - [ ] Unrestrained shadowblight triggers inescapable despair.
- [ ] A Seeker corners the party, forcing them to fight.
    - [ ] A powerful personage reveals their Cult allegiance.
    - [ ] A defeated Seeker reappears with newfound abilities or allies.
    - [ ] An army of cultists sweeps across Valloren culling sanctuaries.
- [ ] The Vessel is lost or stolen.

## TIER 4: FINAL CATACLYSM

Each time you mark a box from this tier, you gain a number of [[Fear]] equal to the number of marked boxes in this tier.

- [ ] A group of Seekers ambushes the party.
- [ ] Valloren is riven by an impassable mire of shadowblight.
- [ ] A tyrant claims the crown of Valloren.
- [ ] An assassin mortally wounds a party member.
- [ ] In pockets of reality, time warps and bends in on itself.
- [ ] A wave of wild magic sweeps across the continent, transforming everything it touches into overgrown, uncanny forms.
- [ ] Whole sanctuaries are terrorized by colossal agents of the earth, raging at its exploitation.
- [ ] The sky turns black forever, plunging Valloren into endless midnight.
- [ ] One by one, sanctuaries slip under the surface of an infinite sea of shadowblight.
- [ ] The veil of death is torn asunder, spewing shambling corpses and unclean spirits into the mortal realm.
    - [ ] The Lovers awaken and begin weaving the Second Sorrow.
        - [ ] The Vessel is destroyed.
