# HIDDEN

While you’re out of sight from all foes and they don’t know where you are, you gain the **Hidden** condition. While Hidden, any rolls against you have disadvantage. After an adversary moves to where they would see you, you move into their line of sight, or you make an attack, you are no longer Hidden.

> **Example:** While scouting a temple to the Fallen Gods and investigating some large statues depicting ancient battles, Rune hears footsteps coming. He declares that he’s going to duck behind the statues and wait for whoever’s approaching to pass. The GM decides that the statues provide ample cover and doesn’t call for a roll. Rune is now considered Hidden. The GM describes snippets of dialogue as two cultists walk right by Rune as he sticks to the shadows, then asks Rune what he wants to do next. If Rune had attacked the cultists as they passed, he’d have lost the Hidden condition after doing so.
