# BASES

Sortable, filterable lists built from each note's properties. Open a base and use its view menu to switch views. Adding or changing a note updates every list that includes it.

## [[Adversaries.base|ADVERSARIES]]

Every adversary, core and Hope & Fear.

- **Views:** All, By tier, By type, Hope & Fear
- **Related notes:** [[Adversaries]]

## [[Environments.base|ENVIRONMENTS]]

Every environment, core and Hope & Fear.

- **Views:** All, By tier, By type, Hope & Fear
- **Related notes:** [[Environments]]

## [[Weapons.base|WEAPONS]]

Every primary and secondary weapon.

- **Views:** All, By tier, Primary Physical, Primary Magic, Secondary, Hope & Fear
- **Related notes:** [[Primary Weapons]], [[Secondary Weapons]]

## [[Armor.base|ARMOR]]

Every armor, with thresholds and base score.

- **Views:** All, Hope & Fear
- **Related notes:** [[Armor]]

## [[Loot.base|LOOT]]

Every item and consumable, plus the random-roll tables.

- **Views:** All, Items, Consumables, Hope & Fear, Random Items Core, Random Items Hope & Fear, Random Consumables Core, Random Consumables Hope & Fear
- **Related notes:** [[Loot]], [[Items]], [[Consumables]], [[Random Item Table]], [[Random Consumable Table]]

## [[Domain Cards.base|DOMAIN CARDS]]

All 210 domain cards.

- **Views:** All, By domain, By level, Arcana, Blade, Bone, Codex, Dread, Grace, Midnight, Sage, Splendor, Valor, By type
- **Related notes:** [[Domains]]

## HOW THEY WORK

- Each stat-block note (adversary, environment, weapon, armor, item, consumable, domain card) carries a few properties at the top. The bases read those.
- The styled card in each note is still the full record; the properties are derived from it.
- Source (`core`, `hope-and-fear` or `homebrew`) is a property, so core and Hope & Fear entries share a base.
