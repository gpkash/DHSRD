# THE SPIRES

Within [[The Sablewood]] there are a number of Spires with attendants that take up the position any time the previous tower tender dies, causing their signal fire to extinguish. To become a Spire Keeper, one must run through the woods, off all known paths, and make it to the unattended Spire to light the signal fire. No one knows the true job of these Keepers, only that someone must always reside in each tower within the forest.

**You might find:**
- Twisting towers of stone that reach beyond the canopy of the Sablewood
- The soft clatter of Spire stones falling from great heights to rest among the undergrowth
- The ozone smell of powerful magic
