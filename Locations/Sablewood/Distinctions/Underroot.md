# UNDERROOT

Because the surface of the uninhabited [[The Sablewood|Sablewood]] is dangerous, many people choose to live in the network of subterranean tunnels carved in and among the roots of the Sablewood trees. Each community keeps the entrance to their underground home secret, and many maps leading to [[Underborne|Underroot]] communities are coded.

**You might find:**
- Winding stairways and tunnels, lit only by faerie lights
- Raucous laughter over meals shared with small communities
- The acrid scent of roots and herbs brewing over a fire
