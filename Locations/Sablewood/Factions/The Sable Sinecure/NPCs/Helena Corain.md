# HELENA CORAIN

*[[Ridgeborne]] [[Human]] — She/Her*
*Direct, Affable, Brave*

**Experience:** Climbing +3, Strike a Deal +2
**Difficulty:** 12

An elderly human that free climbs [[The Titan's Steps]] to collect and sell mountain crab eggs. She's not from around here but commands more respect than most locals of the wood. She can get free lunch just about anywhere she goes.

**Motive:** Reach the top of the Titan's Steps.
