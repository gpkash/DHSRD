# WILL SCILD

*Wanderborne [[Galapa]] — He/Him*
*Unphased, Dry, Fair*

**Experience:** Merchant +2, Retaliation +7
**Difficulty:** 15

A galapa of indeterminate age. Though he moves slowly through [[The Sablewood]] with goods of exceedingly high value, no one dares disturb his journey. He carries no weapon, but he is known to toss a small stone back and forth between his hands.

**Motive:** Find the lost treasure rumored to be in Cradle of the Forgotten Gods.
