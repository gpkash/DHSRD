# XEN, LAPIDARIAN OF THE HIGHBRUSH

*[[Wildborne]] Dwarf — She/Her*
*Cocky, Practical, Discerning*

**Experience:** Rocks +10
**Difficulty:** 8

As wide as she is tall with a shaved head and faceted nails, Xen has an assortment of finely polished and cut emerald stones embedded into her right arm.

**Motive:** Get her hands on a mountain crab's shell to polish and use as armor.
