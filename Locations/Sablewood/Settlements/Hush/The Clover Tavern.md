# THE CLOVER TAVERN

The Clover Tavern is a sight to behold, with six curving stories climbing the trunk of an ancient tree. This is the heart of the community of [[Hush]], always crowded with music and good-natured conversation.

## Customs

- Newcomers to the bar must take off their shoes and hang them over a line that stretches across the bar's ground floor
- Inevitably, by the time visitors leave, their shoes will be shined and filled with small trinkets
