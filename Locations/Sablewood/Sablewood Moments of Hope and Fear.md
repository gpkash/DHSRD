# SABLEWOOD MOMENTS OF HOPE AND FEAR

Roll a d12 or choose from the tables below to describe moments of Hope or [[Fear]] while traveling through [[The Sablewood]].

## Moments of Hope

| d12 | Moment |
|:---:|---|
| 1 | A friendly merchant invites you to their fire and shares a story. |
| 2 | A map of the Briar, only slightly torn. |
| 3 | A fox-bat takes refuge within your coat for the evening. |
| 4 | A young hunter arrives back in town carrying her first kill. |
| 5 | The standing stones of the Forgotten Gods glowing many shades of blue. |
| 6 | A tree branch reaching down to catch you from falling. |
| 7 | A harvest of fresh twilight plums, ripe and pungent. |
| 8 | An ancient wooden chest buried beneath hundreds of years of overgrowth. |
| 9 | A Spire being ascended by its keeper, their nightly hunt over their shoulder. |
| 10 | A lively marketplace full of handcrafted goods and freshly baked pastries. |
| 11 | An ornately decorated carriage pulled by a fleet of horse-goats. |
| 12 | Endless drinks poured over merry music and hearty laughter. |

## Moments of Fear

| d12 | Moment |
|:---:|---|
| 1 | A merchant's cart overturned, wheel spinning, only viscera left behind. |
| 2 | The snapping of twigs directly behind you. |
| 3 | The wind roaring through the canopy, in its echoes reverberates your name. |
| 4 | A set of animalistic eyes watching from just off the path, waiting for the perfect moment to strike. |
| 5 | [[Thistlefolk]] bandits cutting coin purses and disappearing into the underbrush. |
| 6 | A tiger-elk tearing its prey limb from limb. |
| 7 | A wicked smile, then a hand snuffing out your lantern. |
| 8 | Skeletons skewered on tree limbs, old tatters of clothes blowing in the wind. |
| 9 | A pit trap covered in leaves, prepared for its next victim. |
| 10 | A tangle of thorns dripping with fresh blood slowly absorbed. |
| 11 | A shattered sword left behind in pieces, its hilt grown over with moss. |
| 12 | Massive trees uprooted and thrown by something enormous. |
