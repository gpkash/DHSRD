# USING THIS BOOK

This vault is a digital, searchable version of the Daggerheart Core Rulebook organized as an Obsidian vault. It's divided into five chapters.

## CHAPTER ONE: [[PREPARING FOR ADVENTURE]]

Everything needed to make a character: [[Ancestries]], [[Communities]], [[Classes]], [[Domains]], [[Domain Cards]], [[Traits]], and the broader [[World Overview]].

## CHAPTER TWO: [[PLAYING AN ADVENTURE]]

The core game mechanics needed to play a session: [[Action Rolls]], [[Damage Rolls]], the [[The Spotlight|Spotlight]], [[Hope]] and [[Fear]], [[Stress]], [[Domain Cards]], [[Range]], [[Conditions]], [[Downtime]], [[Leveling Up]], and [[Equipment]].

## CHAPTER THREE: [[RUNNING AN ADVENTURE]]

Specifically for the GM. [[GM Principles]], [[GM Best Practices]], [[Pitfalls to Avoid]], [[Core GM Mechanics]]; [[Session Zero and Safety Tools]]; and chapters on [[Running a Session]] and [[Running a Campaign]].

## CHAPTER FOUR: [[ADVERSARIES & ENVIRONMENTS]]

Rules and [[Adversaries|stat blocks for adversaries]] and [[Environments|environments]], plus guidance for [[Choosing Adversaries]], [[Using Adversaries]], and [[Using Environments]] to build your own.

## CHAPTER FIVE: [[CAMPAIGN FRAMES]]

Six pre-built campaign frames — [[The Witherwild]], [[Five Banners Burning]], [[Beast Feast]], [[The Age of Umbra]], [[Motherboard]], and [[Colossus of the Drylands]] — that can structure long-term play or inspire your own.

## NAVIGATING THE VAULT

- **Wiki-links** (`[[Name]]`) jump between pages. Click one to open the target; use the back-arrow to return.
- **Search** (`⌘+O` / `Ctrl+O`) opens any page by name. The Omnisearch plugin adds full-text search.
- **Backlinks** (bottom of each page, or the right-side panel) show every page that references the current one — useful for seeing where a term is used.
- **The graph view** gives a visual map of how topics connect.
- **Folder notes** — each folder has a landing page with the same name (e.g., [[Ancestries]] inside the `Ancestries/` folder). Open the folder note for a table of contents of that section.

## WHAT'S NOT IN THE VAULT

- **The printed cards.** Every card's text is available as a note, but the physical / print-and-play cards themselves come from the official product or the [Daggerheart downloads page](https://www.daggerheart.com/downloads).
- **Character sheets and campaign maps.** These are print-and-play resources; the class-specific sheets are at <https://www.daggerheart.com>.
- **The appendix.** The original Core Rulebook's appendix is reference material (domain card reference, character sheets, maps) that is already distributed throughout this vault or linked to the official downloads.
