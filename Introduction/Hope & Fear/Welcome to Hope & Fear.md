# WELCOME TO HOPE & FEAR

Daggerheart: Hope & Fear *is an expansion that provides additional content for players and GMs who own the* Daggerheart Core Set*.*

## A DAGGERHEART EXPANSION

We're so glad you're here. This book is an expansion, and therefore assumes you own the *Daggerheart Core Set* and are looking for more content to include in your campaigns. Since the game launched, our team has been very excited to continue building for the system, and we hope this expansion helps enhance your Daggerheart games. As always, the golden rule applies to all the work we've done here: Use what brings your table joy, and leave behind what doesn't.

## WHAT'S INCLUDED IN THIS EXPANSION?

*Hope & Fear* provides the following collection of new material to supplement the *Daggerheart Core Set*:

- The **[[Dread]]** domain
- The **[[Assassin|assassin]]**, **[[Brawler|brawler]]**, **[[Warlock|warlock]]**, and **[[Witch|witch]]** classes
- Six **[[Ancestries#HOPE & FEAR ANCESTRIES|ancestries]]** and six **[[Communities#HOPE & FEAR COMMUNITIES|communities]]**
- A set of **[[Transformations|transformations]]** available for characters: the [[Demigod|demigod]], [[Ghost|ghost]], [[Reanimated|reanimated]], [[Shapeshifter|shapeshifter]], [[Vampire (Transformation)|vampire]], and [[Werewolf|werewolf]]
- A collection of **[[Equipment|equipment]]** and **[[Items|loot]]**
- Numerous **[[Adversaries#HOPE & FEAR ADVERSARIES|adversaries]]** and **[[Environments#HOPE & FEAR ENVIRONMENTS|environments]]**
- Four **[[Campaign Frames#HOPE & FEAR CAMPAIGN FRAMES|campaign frames]]**

## HOW SHOULD I IMPLEMENT THESE ELEMENTS?

All aspects of this book are intended as additional options for your table to choose from or use when playing Daggerheart. As always, the GM should decide which elements fit the style of the campaign they're running and offer those options to their players. Just because they're in this book doesn't mean they should automatically be added to every campaign.

If you're playing or running Daggerheart for the first time, we recommend beginning with the core set. The elements included in this expansion are designed to build on the choices and complexity of the base game in a way that might not be as easy to navigate for a first-time GM or player. For example, including all twenty-four ancestry options or all thirteen class options at character creation for a first-time group can feel very overwhelming. Consider implementing new options from *Hope & Fear* after you have at least a few games of Daggerheart under your belt.

## DREAD DOMAIN

Some class options in this book use a domain called Dread. Dread is the domain of nightmares and fear. Those who choose this path can call forth monstrosities, enfeeble their foes, and channel terrifying magic to destroy their enemies. Dread grants its adherents power over forces most are too afraid to employ.

Dread domain cards are included in this expansion, and the [[Dread]] domain features can additionally be found in the appendix on page 180.
