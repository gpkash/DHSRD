> [!adversary-standard]+ **ANCIENT SKELETON**
> *Tier 1 Standard*
>
> *A crumbling skeleton wielding a rusted sword, disturbed from the ground by arcane energy.*
>
> **Difficulty:** 12  |  **Thresholds:** 7/none  |  **HP:** 2  |  **[[Stress]]:** 1
> **ATK:** +0  |  **Rusted Sword:** Melee  |  1d6+1 phy
>
> **FEATURES**
>
> ***Group Attack — Action:*** **Spend a [[Fear]]** to choose a target and spotlight all Ancient Skeletons within Close range of them. Those creatures move into Melee range of the target and make one shared attack roll. On a success, they deal 4 physical damage each. Combine this damage.
>
> *If the ancient skeletons take 6 or less damage, they mark one hit point. Otherwise, they are defeated.*
