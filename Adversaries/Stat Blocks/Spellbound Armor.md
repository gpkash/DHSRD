>

> **FEATURES**
>
> ***Tireless — Passive:*** The Armor can't be forced to mark [[Stress]]. When an effect would cause it to mark [[Stress]], the Armor ignores that part of the effect.
>
> ***Clatter & Recombobulate — Reaction:*** Once per scene when the Armor is defeated, you can **spend a [[Fear]]** to reactivate it. Clear 2 HP and immediately spotlight it.
