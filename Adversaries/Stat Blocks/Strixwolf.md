> [!adversary-skulk]+ **STRIXWOLF**
> *Tier 1 Skulk*
>
> *A wolf-owl pack hunter with wings and a rotating head.*
>
> **Motives & Tactics:** Stalk, Surround, Protect Pack
> <span class="exp-line">**Experience:** Tracker +4</span>
>
> **Difficulty:** 13  |  **Thresholds:** 5/10  |  **HP:** 4  |  **[[Stress]]:** 2
> **ATK:** +2  |  **Bite and Claw:** Melee  |  1d6+3 phy
>
> **FEATURES**
>
> ***Pack Tactics — Passive:*** When making a Bite & Claw attack, if another Strixwolf is also in Melee range of the target, deal **2d8+2** phy damage instead.
>
> ***Powerful Senses — Passive:*** This creature ignores the *Hidden* condition on anyone within Close range.
>
> ***Fly — Action:*** **Mark a [[Stress]]** to make the Strixwolf take flight, increasing their Difficulty by +2 and making their attacks worth +2 damage until the Strixwolf lands.
