> [!adversary-bruiser]+ **CHICKEN-FOOT HUT**
> *Tier 2 Bruiser*
>
> *A tall wooden shack that moves around on giant chicken legs.*
>
> **Motives & Tactics:** Claw, protect master, run and jump, trample
>
> **Difficulty:** 16  |  **Thresholds:** 14/27  |  **HP:** 8  |  **[[Stress]]:** 2
> **ATK:** +2  |  **Clawed Feet:** Very Close  |  2d10+3 phy
> <span class="exp-line">**Experience:** Forests +2</span>
>
> **FEATURES**
>
> ***Fast — Passive:*** The Hut can move up to Far range when spotlighted.
>
> ***Double Strike — Action:*** **Mark a [[Stress]]** to make two standard attacks. If both attacks succeed against the same target, combine the damage.
>
> ***Pin — Reaction:*** When the Hut makes a successful standard attack, you can **mark a [[Stress]]** to temporarily *Restrain* the target. The Hut drags the *Restrained* target when it moves.
