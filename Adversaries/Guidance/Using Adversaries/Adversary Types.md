# ADVERSARY TYPES

An adversary’s type represents the role they play in a conflict. When building encounters, utilizing a mix of types helps create a dynamic and engaging challenge. The adversary types are as follows:

- **[[Bruiser|Bruisers]]** are tough and deliver powerful attacks.
- **[[Horde|Hordes]]** are groups of identical creatures acting together as a single unit.
- **[[Leader|Leaders]]** command and summon other adversaries.
- **[[Minion|Minions]]** are easily dispatched but dangerous in numbers.
- **[[Ranged]]** are fragile in close encounters but can attack from a distance for high damage.
- **[[Skulk|Skulks]]** maneuver and exploit opportunities to ambush opponents.
- **[[Social|Socials]]** present unique challenges to overcome through conversation rather than combat.
- **[[Solo|Solos]]** present a formidable challenge to a whole party, with or without support.
- **[[Standard|Standards]]** are rank-and-file adversaries representative of their fictional group.
- **[[Support|Supports]]** enhance their allies and disrupt their opponents.
